TWO BRANDS, ONE SHOWROOM

Powertrac or Digitrac — which fits you?

Both are built by Escorts Kubota Limited. Powertrac is the proven, fuel-efficient workhorse across a wide HP range. Digitrac is the newer, bold-styled series for farmers who want modern design with serious power.

POWERTRAC · 24 TO 60+ HP

Powertrac

Known for fuel efficiency and value. Euro, ALT, Next and PowerHouse series cover everything from compact orchard work to heavy 4WD haulage.

  • Wide choice: compact, 2WD and 4WD
  • Strong resale and easy parts availability
  • Best for value-focused, all-round farming

DIGITRAC · 45 TO 55 HP

Digitrac

Escorts Kubota's newer, digitally-inspired series with bold black styling, ABC 15-speed gearbox and a 2000 kg Sensi-1 hydraulic lift.

  • Modern styling that stands out
  • High torque, up to 247 Nm
  • Best for rotavator, super seeder & commercial work
